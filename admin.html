1 · Teams

Each team is a pair. Pick two players, or type any name.

2 · Pools

Assign each team to a pool in the Teams list, then generate. Re-generating keeps scores already entered.

3 · Pool play scores

Enter games won by each team. Leave blank until the match is played.

4 · Semifinals, Final, 3rd place and Consolation

Once every pool match has a score, the top two in each pool fill the semifinals and the bottom two fill the consolation semifinals. The final, 3rd place match, consolation final and consolation 3rd place match fill from those results. Both 3rd place matches are first to 4 games. Change any team by hand if you like.

Note on the bracket page

Pro Showcase betting pool

Parimutuel: every bet goes into one pot and the winners split it in proportion to their stake. Open the pool to take bets, close it when the match starts, then pick the winner and settle. Cash changes hands in person.
